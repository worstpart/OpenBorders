# Warship art — image prompt (milestone 2B.6)

The user chose (D-031) to make the warship art with the same image tool that made
`openborder_icon_pack_final/`. Paste the prompt below into that tool; attach
`openborder_icon_pack_final/sheets/sams_walls_silos_missiles.png` and one trade ship
(`vehicles/trade_ships/level_2_ww2/…png`) as style references if the tool accepts images.

Until the sheet arrives the game draws a **labelled stand-in** (the troop-boat sprite tinted
grey). When it arrives, save it as `openborder_icon_pack_final/sheets/warships.png` and the
cutter in `tools/iconPack.mjs` turns it into `warship-1..3.png`.

## Prompt

> A sprite sheet of three warship icons for a strategy game, in exactly the same art style as
> the attached reference icons: detailed painted pixel-art, isometric three-quarter view from
> the upper left, bow pointing to the upper right, soft lighting from the top left, crisp dark
> outlines, rich saturated colours, small and readable at icon size.
>
> One row, three ships, left to right, evenly spaced with plenty of empty space between them:
>
> 1. **Level 1 — WW1 era:** a dreadnought-style battleship, dark grey-blue steel hull with a red
>    waterline stripe, two tall funnels with a little smoke, a tripod mast, two twin gun turrets
>    fore and aft.
> 2. **Level 2 — WW2 era:** a destroyer / light cruiser, light "battleship grey" hull with a
>    red waterline stripe, one raked funnel, a boxy bridge with a radar mast, three gun
>    turrets, small anti-aircraft guns along the deck.
> 3. **Level 3 — Modern era:** a stealth guided-missile frigate, smooth angular pale grey hull
>    with a dark waterline, a faceted superstructure with a flat radar panel, a single sleek
>    gun turret on the bow, vertical missile hatches on the deck, a helicopter pad at the
>    stern.
>
> Same scale and similar length for all three ships (each about as long as the attached trade
> ship). **Transparent background. No water, no waves, no wake, no shadow on the ground. No
> text, no labels, no captions, no flags, no national markings, no numbers on the hull.**
> Nothing overlapping between the ships.
